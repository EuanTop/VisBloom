# Digital art

Start with what the creator wants someone to experience. For an open brief, look for one specific tension, action or relationship the audience can sense. Try a form in which an object, material, composition, sound or gesture actually does something that carries that intent. A still can do this through scale, placement, texture, omission or reading order. Choose the medium from the encounter you want to make, including flat, spatial or mixed work.

When the brief gives you a feeling and leaves the form open, examine the first image that comes to mind. Ask what it leaves unexpressed. Try a second idea in which something different carries the feeling, such as a familiar gesture, an object changing its use, or a detail revealed by looking again. Compare how the viewer would experience them before committing to a scene. Keep this exploration brief for a requested first draft.

For a consequential choice between viable directions, describe what the viewer would actually see, hear or do in each. If the difference depends on how it looks, show small studies. Proceed when the brief is clear or the creator delegates the decision. For a focused revision, begin with the existing artwork and the creator’s latest direction.

Use [precise expression](references/expression.md) when the idea feels generic or when the first draft relies too heavily on its description. Use `npm run --silent visloom -- references find --query "…"` to find specific precedents, movement or material techniques. Read the selected reference or implementation and transfer the relevant decision to this piece. Look up specific tool or material guidance with `skills find` when it can solve a concrete problem.

Render the piece early and **inspect the image with your eyes**. At viewing size, find where attention lands and what the viewer can discover. Up close, inspect edges, shapes, overlap, typography, and the way color and light belong to the surface. With motion or interaction, also look at the actual change that carries the idea. Refine the weakest visible choice before presenting the work. Use [Visloom operations](references/workbench.md) for capture, revision and export.

Let the artwork decide how much text and interface belongs in it. For direct interaction, put the gesture where its result can be felt. Read `artwork-controls` only to implement controls that the creator or audience needs. A loop, a timer, and a seekable timeline are distinct viewing choices. When continuing, reopen the same project and keep choices the creator has accepted. Show the changed artwork so they can respond to it directly.
