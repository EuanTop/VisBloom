# Precise expression

An evocative brief can leave interpretation open while being precise about what occurs in the piece. Locate the experience in an observable choice. What establishes the world? Which element carries the idea? What changes or stays deliberately still? What would the piece lose if that element were swapped for another?

- **An existing rule changes.** Learn the use or behavior the viewer already recognizes, then find the one change that gives it a second meaning. A material can retain the texture and arrangement of the original object while gaining a different behavior. Think through the point of overlap or contact at which the viewer recognizes both readings.
- **A reveal has a cause and a timing.** Decide which detail the viewer must recognize before a later one can change its meaning. A still can use occlusion, distance or the sequence of reading. A moving work can let one action cause the next. Repetition can steady, delay, aggravate or transform the same moment.
- **A material can carry the subject.** Try what survives erasing, how light grazes a surface, or what happens when layers meet or split. Let tests change the idea if the material suggests a clearer way to express it. Avoid taking the first plausible motif as the whole composition.
- **The viewer can complete the event.** Identify what the viewer’s gesture, movement, wait or refusal makes possible. Design feedback in the same visual language as the piece. If the work is already complete as a still or loop, leave room to look.

Treat finish as part of the expression. Decide what the eye follows in the full composition and where detail pays off. Flat color can produce a readable silhouette, directional variation can place light on a surface, and a local highlight can make one material legible. For Chinese and Latin typography, judge the actual scale, density, and weight together. If the words explain an action the image could reveal on its own, try putting that explanation into the artwork’s timing, composition or gesture.

When trying directions, vary what the viewer encounters or how it develops. Keep the most coherent option and let small, focused tests settle refinements. For precedent, the local `references find` index includes links and notes on observable choices. Look at several mechanisms and keep screenshots or notes only as needed for the project.
